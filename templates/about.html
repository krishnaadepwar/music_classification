About the Music Genre Classifier

🎵 Welcome to the Music Genre Classifier! This web app allows you to upload an audio file and uses a deep learning model to predict the genre of the music. Our model is trained on the GTZAN dataset, which includes 10 different genres such as rock, pop, jazz, blues, and more.

🔎 How It Works:

  • Upload an audio file (MP3 format).
  • The app extracts key audio features using Librosa.
  • The features are fed into a deep learning model to predict the genre.
  • The result shows the predicted genre and confidence score!

🚀 Why This App is Special:

  • 🎯 High accuracy using advanced deep learning techniques.
  • ⚖️ Balanced class distribution using SMOTE.
  • 📈 Interactive dashboard to visualize audio analysis.
  • 🌙 Light and dark mode support for better user experience.

🎶 Supported Genres:

  • Blues: A soulful genre with roots in African-American history, featuring deep lyrics and guitar-based instrumentation.
  • Classical: Traditional music often performed by orchestras with complex harmonies and instrumentals.
  • Country: Storytelling-focused music often featuring guitars, fiddles, and heartfelt lyrics.
  • Disco: Dance-oriented music with strong basslines and rhythmic beats, popular in the 1970s.
  • Hip-Hop: A rhythmic and rhyming style that includes rapping and strong beats.
  • Jazz: A genre known for improvisation, swing rhythms, and complex harmonies.
  • Metal: Loud and aggressive music featuring distorted guitars and intense drumming.
  • Pop: Catchy and widely appealing music, often characterized by strong melodies and repetitive hooks.
  • Reggae: A Jamaican genre with offbeat rhythms and socially conscious lyrics.
  • Rock: Guitar-driven music with diverse influences, ranging from soft ballads to hard rock anthems.
